Hedgehog’s Comet

Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026.

Hedgehog’s Comet is a free Japanese revision tool for learners in Aotearoa New Zealand. You can practise without an account. This statement explains what happens to your information.

What we collect and why

Guest practice is saved in your browser: reviewed notes, vocabulary ratings, settings, paragraph work, text drafts and language hints. This lets you return to your practice. Original files are not synced. If you share a practice, its title and reviewed text are included in the link. Anyone who receives the link can open and forward it. Printed OCR and notebook parsing run locally. If you explicitly enable cloud handwriting recognition, a resized copy of the selected image is sent to the operator’s Firebase function and Google Cloud Vision to recognise Japanese handwriting. The app does not persist that image; Google processes it under its service terms. Account-linked daily usage counts are held to enforce quotas.

If you choose an account, Firebase Authentication handles your username-based identifier and password. A synthetic email identifier is generated from your username for authentication; no real email address is requested. The app does not store passwords in Firestore. Firebase stores authentication information, account identifiers and security-related service data. Firestore holds your reviewed text, ratings, settings and drafts to sync them across devices. Supplying this information is optional; without it, guest practice still works.

Who can access it

Your cloud practice is restricted to your signed-in account by database rules. The site operator and authorised infrastructure administrators may have administrative access when needed to operate or troubleshoot the service. Notes are not published to other students or indexed by search engines. Private cloud sync is not end-to-end encryption.

Services and downloads

GitHub Pages hosts the website. Firebase provides accounts, database services and, when enabled, App Check abuse protection. Google Fonts and public software CDNs supply fonts and document readers. Downloading resources sends normal connection information such as IP address and browser details to those providers, who may process information outside New Zealand. Firebase’s database location is selected by the operator when the database is created; other provider services can use different locations.

OneNote extraction, printed-text OCR, romaji generation and English translation run on your device. Cloud handwriting is optional and sends an image off-device as described above. Translation may download models from Hugging Face and software CDNs, or use your browser’s built-in translation model. The app does not send your source text to a translation API. The chosen device speech voice may send spoken text to its voice provider. Check your device settings if you need on-device speech.

Storage, retention and control

Guest data stays until you remove it or clear browser storage. Account data stays until you delete it or delete the account. Signing out leaves an account-specific browser copy for the next sign-in; it is separated from guest practice. If others share your operating-system login, they may still be able to inspect browser storage. For sensitive material, export your work, sign out, and clear this site’s browser data on shared devices.

Use the account panel to export your information, restore a backup, change your password or delete your account and cloud practice. The operator’s service providers may retain security logs or backups under their own retention arrangements after active records are deleted. There is no email password recovery, so keep your password and backups safe.

Learners, families and schools

Use a nickname. Bring only learning material you are allowed to use. Remove other people’s personal information before importing a notebook. Ask your teacher or a parent/caregiver before creating an account if you need help understanding this statement. The app does not ask for your school, legal name, date of birth, location or microphone access. The app includes no advertising, analytics trackers or sale of learner information.

Access, corrections and questions

You can view, edit and export your practice in the app. You may also ask the operator for access to, or correction of, personal information held about you. For privacy enquiries contact the site operator (contact details must be added before public release). Do not post private notes or passwords in a public issue tracker.

This statement will be updated if the service’s data handling changes. Read the current version before choosing cloud sync. For independent guidance, visit the New Zealand Privacy Commissioner.
