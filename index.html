Choose a beginner pack or add words from your Japanese class. Kana is the Japanese writing you’ll hear; the roman letters are a temporary pronunciation hint.

Set a comfortable speed and leave a few seconds to repeat aloud. Japanese long vowels matter: おばさん and おばあさん are different words. Listen for the length.

Can I use this from scratch?

Yes. Begin with Greetings or First kana. Hints show reading and meaning. This is a revision and listening companion, so use your teacher or course for explanations and feedback on pronunciation.

How do I get clear Japanese audio?

Choose a Japanese voice in the settings. If none appears, install a Japanese text-to-speech voice in your device’s language settings and restart your browser. Voice quality varies by device. Try a speed around 0.85× first. Some voices need an internet connection; your device or voice provider may process the spoken text.

Which files can I bring?

MD and TXT; DOCX text; PNG, JPG and JPEG via text recognition; PDFs with selectable text or scanned pages; and ZIP collections of these. ONEPKG notebook exports and ONE sections have a local typed-text reader. Review recovered content carefully: images, handwriting, protected sections and some notebook versions need PDF/DOCX export. Historical revisions and reading order may differ. Handwriting, protected documents and complex layouts may need manual correction. Files are limited to 20 MB each, PDFs to 200 pages, and ZIPs to 60 supported files / 80 MB expanded. Nested ZIPs are skipped.

How do I share a practice with students?

Save your material, open My material, and choose Share. Send the copied link to your students. It opens the material in the Practice Studio without an account. Students can choose Save to My material to keep a copy.

Where is my material stored?

Imports are processed in your browser. Text-recognition and document-reader libraries are downloaded when needed. Reviewed practice text, ratings, drafts and settings are saved in this browser. With an optional free account, they sync privately through Firebase. Guest and account data are separated. Original files are not synced. This is not a backup: export your practice as JSON. Imported practice can be removed in My material.

Keyboard shortcuts

When you aren’t typing: Space starts or pauses, 1 marks Forgot and 2 marks Got it when the rating prompt appears, and the right arrow moves to the next item. All controls also work by keyboard with Tab and Enter.
