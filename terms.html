Use your learning material responsibly

Import only content you own or have permission to use. Do not import classmates’ personal information without appropriate permission. You retain your rights to your material. The service may process and store the material only as needed to provide the features you choose, including cloud sync. This is not permission to publish or sell your notes.

Learning hints need a human check

Speech voices, romaji, OCR, notebook extraction and machine translations can be wrong. OneNote extraction may recover older revisions or change reading order. Review imported text and ask your teacher about unclear meanings or pronunciation. Paragraph variations substitute only the words you select; you are responsible for checking that word forms, meaning and grammar still fit. The app is a practice companion and does not certify learning or replace teaching.

Your account and backups

Choose a unique password, keep it private and sign out on shared devices. Usernames are case-insensitive. No real email is collected, and email password recovery is unavailable. Export backups regularly. The app cannot guarantee permanent data storage or uninterrupted access. You can delete your account and cloud practice from the account panel.

The operator may restrict misuse to protect learners and keep the service available. Feature limits and infrastructure can change; the service is intended to remain free for learners.
